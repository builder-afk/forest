## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, athletic commerce aesthetic tailored for premium sports nutrition, functional supplements, and elite training fuel. The visual character merges clean technical precision with raw athletic velocity: high-contrast surfaces, razor-sharp typography, and targeted bursts of electric energy.

The personality balances scientific authority with motivational momentum:
- **Tone:** Uncompromising, scientific, kinetic, performance-driven.
- **Target Audience:** Athletes, strength practitioners, endurance competitors, and active health optimizers seeking clean formulations and uncompromising quality.
- **Emotional Response:** Invigorating focus, confidence in efficacy, urgency to train, and absolute trust in product authenticity.

The interface combines **High-Contrast Bold Structuralism** with **Modern Kinetic Minimalism**. Crisp white surfaces establish clinical cleanliness and legibility, anchored by deep carbon graphite neutrals and energized by high-visibility volt and citrus orange accents. Dense, punchy typography commands attention while generous, disciplined whitespace ensures dense nutritional specifications and ingredient transparency remain digestible.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-visibility, technical athletic accents staged against clinical white and deep carbon surfaces.

### Primary (`#D4FF00` - Electric Volt)
The core brand signature. Used deliberately for primary calls-to-action, high-velocity promotion banners, key achievement metrics, and active selection indicators. When pairing with text, always set against dark neutral `#0F1115` for maximum contrast and WCAG AA compliance.

### Secondary (`#FF5722` - Dynamic Citrus)
Represents kinetic warmth, metabolic energy, and targeted urgency. Applied to flash drops, pre-workout identifiers, warning states, and high-priority nutrition badges (e.g., "High Stim", "Limited Run").

### Tertiary (`#00E5FF` - Kinetic Cyan)
Provides technical contrast. Dedicated to hydration, electrolyte profiles, micronutrient callouts, recovery states, and laboratory certification tags (e.g., "Informed-Sport", "Third-Party Tested").

### Neutral Palette
- **Base Canvas:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` for product grids, modal backdrops, and input fields.
- **Surface Secondary:** Subtle cool off-white `#F4F6F8` for page scaffolding, table rows, and secondary card containers.
- **Surface Subtle:** `#EAEFF4` for dividers, progress tracks, and inactive chip borders.
- **Border Default:** `#E2E7ED` for crisp card separation and data outlines.
- **Text Muted:** `#5A626F` for secondary nutritional details, metadata, and disclaimer text.
- **Neutral Core / Text Dominant (`#0F1115` - Deep Carbon):** Heavy, near-black slate used for display headlines, primary buttons on light surfaces, badges, and footer containers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines geometric authority with athletic punch:
- **Headlines & Display (`Outfit`):** Characterized by tight letter tracking, bold strokes, and wide-aperture forms. Weights `700` through `800` deliver immediate commercial presence on banner takeovers and nutritional macros.
- **Body Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for high legibility across dense supplement facts panels, ingredient breakdowns, customer reviews, and clinical dosing guidelines.
- **Labels & Badges (`Outfit` with heavy caps):** Used across macro indicators (e.g., `30G PROTEIN`, `0G SUGAR`, `CREATINE MONOHYDRATE`), batch testing tags, and categorical pill labels to establish a technical, military-athletic rigor.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built around a dynamic 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate dense product inventories, comparison charts, and editorial athlete stories.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, max-width `1440px`, margins `3rem` (`48px`), gutters `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, margins `2rem` (`32px`), gutters `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, margins `1rem` (`16px`), gutters `0.75rem` (`12px`).

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to an 8-point base module. Hero sections and campaign launches expand to `space-xl` (`2.5rem` / `40px`) or multiples thereof (`80px`), while transactional e-commerce panels (nutrition facts, SKU selector swatches, checkout breakdown) tighten strictly using `space-xs` to `space-md` for high data density and fast scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Tonal Boundary Precision** combined with **Hyper-Subtle Directional Shadow Drops**. The system avoids floating or murky gradients; edges remain crisp, clinical, and tactile.

### Layer Stack
1. **Level 0 (Floor):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` or Light Slate `#F4F6F8`.
2. **Level 1 (Product Cards & Content Tiles):** Raised using a 1px solid border `#E2E7ED` paired with `box-shadow: 0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.04), 0px 6px 16px rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Active States):** Transitioning elevation triggers a crisp boundary accent (e.g., border color shifts to `#0F1115` or `#D4FF00`) and a defined lift: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.06), 0px 16px 32px rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Nutrition Bars, Cart Drawers, Filters):** Backdrop blur of `16px` on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` with an anchored bottom border `rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.08)` and `box-shadow: 0px 10px 40px rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.12)`.
5. **Level 4 (Nutritional Modals & Diagnostic Drawers):** Backdrop dimming in `rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.65)` with foreground containers casting `0px 24px 64px rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft Angularity (`roundedness: 1`)** architecture. This conveys modern industrial engineering and precision packaging rather than toy-like or consumer-casual aesthetics.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Checkboxes, form fields, flavor selection tags, tab indicators, and macro stat containers.
- **Container Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Product cards, nutrition facts trays, promo banners, and modal dialogs.
- **Exceptions (Full Pill Radius / `9999px`):** Exclusively reserved for high-energy status pills (`label-caps`), dietary chips (e.g., `GLUTEN-FREE`, `ISOLATE`), and primary floating checkout triggers to create an instant optical contrast against rectilinear product cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic CTA:** Surface `#D4FF00`, text `#0F1115` in `Outfit Bold`. Hover triggers a crisp 2px scale translation and `#0F1115` drop shadow. Focused states feature a `2px solid #0F1115` outline offset by `2px`.
- **Secondary Athletic Dark:** Surface `#0F1115`, text `#FFFFFF`. On hover, surface shifts to `#1C2028` with an inset border highlight.
- **Tertiary Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #E2E7ED`, text `#0F1115`. On hover, border shifts to `#0F1115`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category & Flavor Chips:** Compact, border `1px solid #E2E7ED`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F1115`. Active state: background `#0F1115`, text `#D4FF00`, border color `#0F1115`.
- **Nutritional Macro Badges:** High-contrast pill badges utilizing `label-caps`. Electric volt background `#D4FF00` for primary protein metrics; dynamic citrus `#FF5722` with white text for caffeine/stim callouts; kinetic cyan `#00E5FF` with dark text for hydration/electrolyte highlights.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white surface, 1px `#E2E7ED` border, 8px radius.
- **Product Imagery:** Positioned against an unclipped, high-contrast `#F4F6F8` display pedestal with subtle bottom drop shadow.
- **Macro Overlay Grid:** Horizontal micro-strip directly below product title showing key metrics: `PROTEIN | 25g`, `BCAAs | 5.5g`, `CALORIES | 120`.
- **Action Strip:** Quick-add flavor carousel followed by a full-width high-contrast Add button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** 4px radius, 48px height, 1.5px border `#E2E7ED`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus state: border `#0F1115`, accompanied by a zero-blur 2px volt glow ring.
- **Serving & Frequency Toggles:** High-density segmented control bars with white cards sliding over a `#F4F6F8` recessed container track.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20px square with 3px radius. Unchecked: 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border. Checked: background `#0F1115`, border `#0F1115`, with a `#D4FF00` checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular, with an energetic `#FF5722` or `#0F1115` concentric ring indicator on selection.

### Supplement Nutrition Facts Panel
- Standardized tabular UI styled like an engineered laboratory readout: black header bar with bold white tracking, dense alternating row bands in `#FFFFFF` and `#F4F6F8`, heavy black borders separating core macro clusters, and clean percentage badges along the right-hand column.
